## v1.6.8  ·  2026-09-03 10:55 PM ET

Two things the app already knew and never said. What you pour against what you own: 344 bottles and a pour log, and nothing had ever compared them. The shelf says what was bought, the log says what gets reached for, and Home now says the difference in numbers when there is enough log to carry an opinion. It matters for buying, because a category owned deeply and poured rarely is not a gap however thin it looks beside something else, and one poured constantly off a narrow shelf is a gap however many bottles sit nearby. The two directions take different evidence: over-poured is a claim about the pours, so seven evenings out of eight from two bottles counts, while under-poured is a claim about the shelf and needs eight bottles before it means anything. And the bottles you keep buying, which is a stronger statement than a star: the stocking rule is a backup for anything worth not running out of, so a whisky owned more than once is one already decided on. A bottle finished still counts, because buying the same whisky three times and drinking two of them is the strongest version of the signal and counting only what is on the shelf now would erase exactly that case. Fourteen of them on this shelf.
